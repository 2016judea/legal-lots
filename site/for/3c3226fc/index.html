A lot counts if it is zoned NCC, RB, SC, VB and 23 planned-development zones; at least 1,000 ft from a K-12 school; at least 500 ft from a day care; at least 500 ft from a treatment facility; at least 500 ft from a park attraction for kids; at least 1,000 ft from an existing cannabis shop.
