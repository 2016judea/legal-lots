A lot counts if it is zoned B-2, B-4, C-2, C-3, C-5, TI, CX-2, LX, CR-1 (or B-1, C-1, C-4, HX-R with a conditional use permit); at least 1,000 ft from a school; at least 300 ft from a treatment facility; at least 300 ft from a park attraction for kids, measured property line to property line.
