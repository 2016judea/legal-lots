This is a request for public data under Minn. Stat. 13.03 subd. 3.

Minn. Stat. 342.20 subd. 2, as amended by 2026 Minn. Laws c.123 s.30, makes the following public for every cannabis business licence applicant: "the applicant's name and designated address," "data disclosing the ownership and control of the applicant," "data showing the legal possession of the premises where the business will operate," and the status of the application.

I'm asking for that data, for every cannabis applicant whose current status is preliminarily approved or qualified:

1. Applicant name, and application ID (e.g. DIS-A25-000080)
2. Licence type (retailer, microbusiness, mezzobusiness, and so on)
3. Designated address
4. Application status, and the date it reached that status
5. Whether a premises has been identified, if the Office records that

I am not asking for social equity status, which subd. 2(b) excludes, or for any data the Office classifies as nonpublic. If a record mixes the two, I'll take it with those fields redacted.

A spreadsheet export (CSV or Excel) is ideal. If that's easier to produce for one licence type first, retailer and mezzobusiness are the most useful, and a partial answer is a useful answer here.

Please let me know before doing anything that would carry a cost over $50.
